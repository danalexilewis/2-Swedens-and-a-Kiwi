- Benji — author’s brother; referenced in relation to Uber / platform or gig work, and potentially disability / capacity-based scheduling. Details in archive are still thin; do not invent platform name, medical facts, timeline, or outcomes beyond what the author explicitly records.

## Risk notes

- Platform-work failures can invite dispute about causes (health, algorithm, local market, personal choice).

Benji illustrates the question; research must carry the generalisation.

## Avoid

- Publishing medical or diagnostic detail the author has not approved
- Implying Uber (or any named platform) “failed because of” Benji’s disability without his framing
- Treating one family story as representative of all disabled gig workers
- Softening into “Uber didn't work for my brother” as the whole point — the interesting question is flexibility without full risk transfer
