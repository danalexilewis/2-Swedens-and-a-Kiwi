## Findings (numbered, most load-bearing first)

1. **Swedish “ownership” is three tenure types, not freehold only.** SCB defines boendeform as hustyp × upplåtelseform: **äganderätt** (privately owned, often småhus), **bostadsrätt** (tenant-owned cooperative; register includes subletters), **hyresrätt** (rental). Folkbokförd ≠ ägare. National 2024/25: 38% of households in småhus äganderätt; 29% flerbostadshus hyresrätt; 21% flerbostadshus bostadsrätt (SCB).

2. **Young adults cluster in flerbostadshus hyresrätt/bostadsrätt, not småhus äganderätt.** SCB 2025 by age: ages 20–24 — 35.3% flerbostadshus hyresrätt, 17.5% bostadsrätt, 30.6% småhus äganderätt (+10.2% specialbostad, incl. student). Ages 25–29 — 40.7% hyresrätt, 26.0% bostadsrätt, 20.9% småhus äganderätt. Peak småhus äganderätt is ~50s–60s (~55%).

3. **Cohort entry to owned housing has weakened.** Register study (single-family ownership): at age 35, 1973 cohort ~38% men / 41.6% women owned; 1983 cohort ~28.9% / 32.4% — “considerable decrease” for younger cohorts (Enström et al., *Review of Economics of the Household*, 2026). Parents’ homeownership became a stronger predictor of first-time ownership as market tightened (Enström Öst, *Urban Studies*, 2011; IFFS working paper on tenure choice).

4. **Parental capital is load-bearing for purchase, especially bostadsrätt.** ICA Banken *Pengakollen* 2025 (19–29): **41%** of young bostadsägare got parental economic help to buy; **36%** of non-owners doubt they can buy within five years. Skandia bank data: **25%** of customers under 30 have a parent as **medlåntagare** (17% Oct 2016). SBAB/TNS Sifo 2017 (18–26 owners): **23%** financed via parent/släkting (16% in 2009); own mortgage share fell 54%→46%. Hyresgästföreningen 2025 (20–27): among bostadsrätt residents, share ever receiving purchase help rose sharply in 2025 wave (chart to 66%; **method change 2025 — compare years cautiously**).

5. **Living with parents = blocked exit, not cultural preference.** Hyresgästföreningen 2025: **21%** of 20–27 (204,000) live with parents; **90%** of those want to move (184,000 “ofrivilligt hemmaboende”). Mean move-out age now **21** (was 19 in 1970s). FI 2018: **42%** of 16–29 lived with parents (Eurostat). ICA 2025: **39%** still at home say they cannot afford to leave; **53%** pay nothing for food/rent at home.

6. **First-hand hyresrätt is the main independent exit — when available.** Hyresgästföreningen 2025: **~7/10** young adults have “eget boende”; **27%** of all 20–27 hold first-hand hyresrätt (most common single category); **21%** at parents’. 2017 report: among those who left home, **35.1%** in first-hand hyresrätt vs **28.8%** in any owned form combined. Preferences: 40% want bostadsrätt, 30% småhus, 25% hyresrätt; ≤2% want andrahand/inneboende.

## Unknowns

- No continuous official series since 2000 on “established via owned housing or primary rental” (FI 2018 flags this gap).
- Hyresgästföreningen 2025 method change may inflate hemmaboende / parental-help trends vs prior waves.
- Skandia/ICA/SBAB figures are bank/survey samples, not population registers; medlåntagare ≠ kontantinsats ≠ gåva.
- SCB folkbokföring understates insecure andrahand/inneboende (not own address).
- No 2024–26 national register estimate of parental transfers into bostadsrätt purchases (gåva/lån/medlåntagare split).
