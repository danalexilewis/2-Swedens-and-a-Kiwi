## Findings (numbered, most load-bearing first)

1. **Membership-cost step-change from ~2003.** Treasury (WP 06/03): median house price ÷ average household income was stable until 2003, then entered a sustained upward trend; by 2006 it was “easily the highest on record.” HUD CHAI (2012–2022): deposit affordability index **−40%** while house prices rose **143%** vs median disposable income **+46%** — entry cost detached from wages over a generation.
2. **Baseline era was ~4–5× income, not today’s 7–10×.** March 2001 national median multiple **4.6** (Auckland **5.1**); Massey (2018) notes 1990s norms around **4×** wages. CoreLogic/Cotality: national value-to-income **5.8** (2004) → peak **10.1** (Q4 2021); long-term average **6.8** (since Q1 2004); **7.3** (Q4 2024), **7.2** (Q4 2025).
3. **Auckland led and stayed stretched.** Stats NZ *Housing in Aotearoa: 2020*: Auckland mid-2020 median sales price ≈ **11.5×** median household disposable income ($900k / $78.5k); Wellington **9.5×**, Canterbury **~7×**. CoreLogic Q4 2024 city long-term averages: Auckland **7.9**, Tauranga **8.6**, Wellington **6.5**, Christchurch **6.8** (vs lower historical baselines).
4. **Rent burden rose in the 1990s and stayed high — unlike the post-2021 purchase retreat.** Stats NZ HES (via *Housing in Aotearoa: 2020*): share of renters spending **>30%** of income on housing rose from **<20%** (1988) to **~45%** within a decade, **>40%** by 2019. Stats NZ news (Jun 2022 year): **1 in 4** renting households spent **>40%** of disposable income on housing; average weekly rent **+93%** over 15 years vs mortgage payments **+48.8%**. CoreLogic: national rent-to-income **24.6%** (2004) → **28.4%** (Q4 2024), “record high” vs long-term **26%**.
5. **House prices ≠ CPI.** Stats NZ: CPI tracks **new-build** housing cost **excluding land**; Stats NZ does **not** produce an official existing-house price index. *Housing in Aotearoa: 2020*: “house prices have been rising at a faster rate than wages over the past five years.” DPMC/Stats NZ (2008, cited in home-ownership report): real prices **+~80%** Mar 2002–Mar 2007 alone.
6. **OECD cross-check (disposable income per head).** OECD via CEIC: standardised price-income ratio **57.9** (Mar 1987) → peak **176.6** (Dec 2021) → **125.2** (Dec 2025), where **100** = long-run average — still **~25%** above historical norm after correction.

## Options / forks this opens

- **Purchase vs rent spine:** Use price-to-income for “buying membership” (2003 break, 2021 peak, partial retreat) and HES/CoreLogic rent-overburden for “renting membership” (1990s step-up, flat at stress levels since ~2007).
- **National vs Auckland:** National multiples understate Auckland; pair national OECD/HUD series with Auckland 11.5× (2020) or CoreLogic regional tables.
- **Gross vs disposable:** CoreLogic uses gross median household income; Treasury/HUD/Stats NZ HES use disposable — do not mix without flagging.
