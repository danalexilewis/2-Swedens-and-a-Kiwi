# W1 — Sweden: housing cost trajectories (price-to-income / rent burden)

## Findings (numbered, most load-bearing first)

1. **Owner entry cost rose far faster than incomes (1990s–2021), then eased.** OECD price-to-income for Sweden (2015=100) peaked at **113.7 in 2021** (record high; trough **51.0 in 1993**). By **2024: 94.4** — back near the 2015 base. OECD also classifies Sweden among countries with the **largest real house-price gains 2005–2019** (≥50 index points on 2015=100).
2. **Villa/small-house prices: long boom, 2021 peak, partial correction.** SCB FASTPI for permanent small houses (1981=100, whole country): **538 (2010) → 985 (2021 peak) → 930 (2024)**; **843 in 2020**. Riksbank (2016): house prices **doubled in real terms since 1995**; household debt ~**180%** of disposable income.
3. **Reported housing-cost *share* of income is surprisingly flat — but owner-occupied houses ticked up.** SCB Hushållens boendeutgifter (median): all tenures **21%→23%** (2015→2024). Hyresrätt steady **~27–28%**; bostadsrätt **~21%**; ägt småhus **15%→18%** (largest move). SCB: share “relatively constant” despite pandemic, price swings, and rate hikes.
4. **Hyresrätt (bruksvärde stock): low annual hikes for years, then a shock.** SCB negotiated-stock increases: **0.8% (2016–17)** → **4.1% (2023)** → **5.0% (2024)** — **“highest since 1993.”** Average 3-room rent: **7,347 kr/mo (2020) → 8,641 kr/mo (2024)** (+18% in four years).
5. **Two-tier hyresrätt market inside the regulated stock.** SCB 2024 by build year (3-room): **7,885–8,508 kr/mo** (1941–2000 stock) vs **11,528 kr/mo** (2011–2020). **New-build hyresrätt 2024:** **2,326 kr/kvm/yr** (+13.2% y/y); 3-room **12,177 kr/mo** — well above stock average.
6. **Bostadsrätt entry ≠ hyresrätt rent path.** SCB new production 2024: average **insats 3,659,000 kr** (71 kvm); 3-room **månadsavgift 5,600 kr**. SCB has apartment sales stats from **2000**; no national bostadsrätt price index like FASTPI for villas. Valueguard HOX (hedonic, ~95% of broker sales; used by banks/authorities) covers bostadsrätt from **2005** — public series largely paywalled since 2023.
7. **Young entrants / new borrowers carried peak burden.** Riksbank/FI (2021): autumn 2020 new mortgagors had **highest loan-to-value and debt-to-income since FI surveys began (2012)**. Bostadsfakta/SCB: owner housing costs peak for households **aged 30–39** (purchase + amortisation counted as housing cost).

## Options / forks this opens

- **Purchase-price story vs cash-flow story:** OECD/Riksbank PTI and FASTPI support “children pay more to buy”; SSE/Riksbank user-cost literature argues low rates made *ongoing* owner costs look cheap until 2022–23 — chapter must pick which “membership cost” lens (entry ticket vs monthly burden).
- **Tenure fork:** Hyresrätt stock rents rose slowly then caught inflation (bruksvärde negotiations); **queue-holders** vs **new-build / young movers** face different trajectories. Bostadsrätt/villa = price-index boom + leverage spike at entry.
- **Generational fork:** Flat *average* boendeutgiftsprocent hides **30–39 owner peak** and **low-income hyresgäst ~40%** (Bostadsfakta citing SCB quintiles) — “rich parents’ kids” vs “stuck in hyresrätt” may be cohort/tenure slices, not the national median.
