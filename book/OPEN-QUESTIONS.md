# Open Questions

Research and framing questions that are not yet answered. Do not pretend they are settled.

## Capital-class thesis

- Which load-bearing statistics best show ownership becoming more decisive than income for housing security in each country?
- How should “unconstrained” be illustrated without overstating (e.g. existing taxes and regulations that are too weak)?

## Housing

- Who can buy without parental help, by cohort and city?
- How has ownership shifted by age?
- What does rental security look like in each country?
- How much of income goes toward housing?
- What is the relationship between city property and rural property?
- Are people being encouraged to treat housing as retirement infrastructure?
- What is the history and tenure model of Poseidon housing in Guldheden (Göteborg)?
- What exactly does a Swedish förstahandskontrakt / allmännytta tenancy guarantee versus private secondary arrangements?
- What are the formal terms of major Swedish rental intermediary platforms (confirm names; fee and late-payment penalty structures)?
- How do NZ Tenancy Tribunal remedies and typical outcomes compare on landlord failure cost?
- Does New Zealand have a tenancy-union analogue with real enforcement teeth?
- What NZ institutions (if any) approximate secure modest rental at scale (Kāinga Ora, community housing, etc.) — and where do they fall short of the Poseidon-like function?
- How much of recent New Zealand household wealth growth came from land rather than buildings?
- How do public infrastructure and planning decisions capitalize into nearby land values in NZ and Swedish cities?

## Monetary policy and assets

- What was predictable at the time of emergency interventions, and what became clear later?
- How should consumer inflation be distinguished from asset-price inflation in the narrative?
- What is distinctive about New Zealand’s housing-heavy financial system versus Sweden’s debt, property, and low-rate dynamics?

## Tax preferences

- How large is the practical benefit of NZ property-gain treatment versus Swedish ISK and related capital treatment for high-capital households?
- What would “sufficiently capped” look like without collapsing into a full tax treatise?
- How much of New Zealand’s current tax revenue comes from labour income, company income, consumption, rates, and property-related gains?
- How does the absence of a general recurring tax on unimproved land-value increases interact with rates and sale rules? (verify before publication)

## Inheritance and generations

- When does inheritance typically arrive relative to the ages when housing and family formation decisions are made?
- How to separate paper wealth from liquid wealth in the argument?
- How does land-value taxation interact with the book’s argument about inheritance?

## Productivity and risk

- How many businesses never exist because potential founders cannot risk losing access to housing? (Hard to measure; what proxies exist?)
- New Zealand’s scaling problem and Sweden’s conformity/employment structures — which facts are load-bearing?
- How much NZ bank lending is secured against residential and commercial land compared with productive business lending?
- Does preferential treatment of land/housing materially divert capital from productive business investment? (causal claim — research needed)

## Fertility and family

- What evidence supports a qualified link between housing insecurity and fertility decisions?
- How to keep the personal claim strong while not overclaiming national causation?

## Migration and brain drain

- Are younger or highly skilled Swedes leaving? Where? Permanently or temporarily?
- Are housing, wages, taxation, culture, or career opportunities material causes?
- Is Sweden suffering a brain drain, or broader European labor mobility?
- Which groups are entering as others leave?
- For New Zealand, how strong is the Australia comparison on skills and housing?

## Politics and management

- At what points did distributional effects become clear enough that “failure to correct course” is a fair charge?

## Institutions worth keeping

- Which Swedish and New Zealand institutions still deliver broad security or mobility and should be defended rather than dismantled?
- How should a “Poseidon-like” proposal for NZ be specified without naive institutional transplant?
- How should a Swedish-style school-meals function be specified for NZ (kitchens, staffing, district governance) without naive transplant?
