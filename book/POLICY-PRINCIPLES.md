# Policy Principles

Working principles for proposals later in the book. Not a full policy platform yet.

## Orientation

The book argues against denial and unconstrained intergenerational inequality. Proposals should follow from that diagnosis, not from a desire to punish success or romanticize the past.

## Working principles

1. **Correct course once distributional effects are clear.** Emergency decisions may have been reasonable; leaving the gains permanently compounding is the failure.
2. **Equal access is not equal benefit** when access to capital is radically unequal. Prefer mechanisms that are capped, progressive, or otherwise limited in how far ownership advantages compound.
3. **Work should be able to buy security.** A middle-class income should still be able to purchase a middle-class life: stable home, room for children, independence, risk-taking capacity, retirement path.
4. **Housing is membership, not only an investment.** Policy that treats housing primarily as retirement infrastructure for owners transfers the cost of membership onto non-owners.
5. **Skill and structural advantage coexist.** Do not design policy as if every fortune were undeserved, or as if every fortune were purely earned.
6. **Preserve institutions worth keeping.** Criticism of Shared Failure is not a mandate to burn everything that still works. See `topics/institutions-worth-keeping.md`.
7. **Predictions are not mandates.** Proposals should be labelled as proposals; forecasts as forecasts.
