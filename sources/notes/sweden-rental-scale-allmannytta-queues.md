## Plain-language summary

Sweden has about **2.05 million** hyresrätter. Roughly **41%** (~846,000) are allmännytta; about **59%** are privately owned. Allmännytta is only ~**16%** of all dwellings. Vacancy immediately available to let was **1.3%** (1 Sep 2024). The binding constraint is **access** to first-hand contracts: Stockholm average wait ~**9 years** (city 12.4; inner city ~21); Göteborg Boplats ~**7 years**. Storgöteborg has about **half** of local rental in allmännytta — denser than the national 41%. Poseidon (~29k) / Framtiden (~76k) are a high-density local version of the model, not the national average. Do **not** invent a national andrahand stock count.

## Useful facts

- ~2.05m hyresrätter; allmännytta ~41% of hyresrätt / ~16% of all dwellings; private ~59% of hyresrätt.
- Vacancy 1.3% (20,246 immediate-to-let, 1 Sep 2024).
- Stockholm 2025: ~895k registered; avg 9.0 years ordinary first-hand.
- Göteborg Boplats 2025: ~308k registered; avg ~6.8 years; 89% of mediated units allmännyttiga.
- Storgöteborg ~half local hyres market municipal; Storstockholm ~40%; Stormalmö ~⅓.
