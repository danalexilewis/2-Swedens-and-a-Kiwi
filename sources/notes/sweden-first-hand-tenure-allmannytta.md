## Plain-language summary

A **förstahandskontrakt** is a direct residential lease with the property owner. Under Jordabalken chapter 12 it carries strong **besittningsskydd** (security of tenure) from day one: the landlord cannot end the tenancy at will; the tenant has a right to prolongation except for listed grounds (serious breach, demolition/major rebuild, etc.), and disputes go to the rent tribunal (hyresnämnden). Rent for ordinary hyresrätt is constrained by the use-value / collective negotiation system (bruksvärde), not pure market asking.

That legal package is **not exclusive to municipal companies**. Private landlords can also grant first-hand hyresrätt with the same tenure protection.

**Andrahand** and private-owner letting are weaker. Sveriges Domstolar: a second-hand tenant of a hyresrätt gets (limited) besittningsskydd only after more than two continuous years, and generally cannot stay if the first-hand tenant returns. Letting of a privately owned home / bostadsrätt under privatuthyrningslagen: **no** besittningsskydd.

**Allmännytta** (Allbolagen 2010:879) defines municipal housing companies that mainly manage rental dwellings, promote municipal housing supply, and offer tenant influence — run on **businesslike principles** (affärsmässiga principer), with limits on value transfers to the municipality. Poseidon is Göteborg’s largest municipal landlord (~28,876 apartments; ~60,000 residents; Framtiden group owned via Stadshus AB / City of Gothenburg). Poseidon states it is self-financed and that profits are mostly reinvested in the business.

## Useful facts

- Förstahand = direct lease with owner; full hyreslag protection (JB 12 kap.), including besittningsskydd from day one (Hyresgästföreningen; JB 12:46).
- Landlord needs valid grounds to break tenure; hyresnämnden decides disputes.
- Ordinary rents negotiated / compared under bruksvärde (JB 12:55); collective negotiation with tenant organisation is the normal path (Sveriges Allmännytta).
- Andrahand of hyresrätt: limited protection only after >2 years; never a right to take over the first-hand contract (Hyresgästföreningen; Domstolar).
- Private home / bostadsrätt letting: no besittningsskydd (Domstolar; privatuthyrningslagen).
- Allbolagen 1 §: municipal control + housing-supply purpose + tenant influence; 2 §: businesslike principles; 3 §: capped value transfers to municipality.
- Poseidon: municipal allmännytta; ~28,876 hyresrätter; self-financed; profits mostly reinvested (company self-description, retrieved 2026-08-07).
- Framtiden group (Poseidon + sisters): about every fourth Gothenburger in allmännytta; ~half of Göteborg’s ~310,000 dwellings are hyresrätter, of which about half are Framtiden-owned (Poseidon 2025 annual report summary language — verify exact wording before load-bearing use of the “every fourth” line).
