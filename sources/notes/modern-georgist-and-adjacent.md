## Plain-language summary

Tideman offers modern economic analysis of efficient taxes on land (including sale-value vs rental-value assessment). Doucet is an accessible explainer of Georgist ideas and objections for ordinary readers — not the primary academic authority. Prosper Australia is an advocacy organisation for modern Australian Georgism and land-value taxation — pair with official and academic sources. Cameron Murray is an Australian housing economist and critic of simplistic housing narratives; useful partly because he challenges orthodoxies on both sides — do **not** treat him as an unquestioning Georgist advocate.

## Useful facts

- Tideman: modern efficiency arguments for land taxes  
- Doucet: communication and worked objections  
- Prosper: advocacy framing and Australian debate history  
- Murray: stress-tests of capitalization / supply / development claims
