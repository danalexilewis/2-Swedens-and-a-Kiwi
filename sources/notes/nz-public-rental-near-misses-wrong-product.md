## Plain-language summary

New Zealand’s largest “public rental” system is **IRRS social housing** (Kāinga Ora + CHPs): MSD **serious housing need** plus income/asset tests; Housing Register / Social Allocation System triage; rent generally ~**25% of net income** with Crown paying the market gap. That is a **welfare / residual-need product** — necessary for some, **wrong product** for a Poseidon/hyresrätt-like starter for middle-income or ordinary young people. Social housing is also thin vs peers (~3.8% of stock; 14.4 per 1,000 vs OECD 32.7), but eligibility design is the load-bearing claim. **Te Kāinga** (Wellington) is closer to the missing function (not IRRS; long-term stable leases; priority includes non-IRRS-eligible) but remains city-only and small (~472 programme apartments; target 1,500 by 2033) versus Poseidon’s ~29k.

## Useful facts

- ~87,000 IRRS / government-funded social places (KO ~73k; CHPs ~14k) — Te Waihanga Nov 2025.
- Social housing ≈3.8% of stock; 14.4 / 1,000 people vs OECD 32.7.
- Eligibility: serious housing need + income/asset tests (MSD; figures time-sensitive).
- IRRS: ~25% of net income; HUD pays market gap.
- Te Kāinga: ~472 programme apartments (WCC); target 1,500 by 2033; not IRRS.
- Poseidon contrast: ~28,876 apartments — S10.

## Claims it supports

- Chapter fact #5: NZ near-misses fall short primarily by **product design (need gate)**, secondarily by scale.
- Proposal: copy Poseidon *function*, do not rebrand IRRS as that function.
- Te Kāinga = existence proof of right product class at wrong scale.
