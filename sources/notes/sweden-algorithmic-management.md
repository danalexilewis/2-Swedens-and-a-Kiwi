## Plain-language summary

ALGOSH studies algorithmic management in Swedish workplaces (including transport, retail, warehousing). Working note: effects on conditions depend on technology, implementation, and context; stronger local union organisation associated with more influence over digital systems introduced. Selberg has written on Sweden’s first collective bargaining agreement for food-delivery riders and implications for platform work, algorithmic management, and the Swedish labour model.
