## How to use

- Capture the scene in the author’s words where possible
- Tag related topics and possible chapters
- Mark claim type (usually `personal-experience`)

## Scene: House or company

**Claim type:** personal-experience  
**Topics:** `productivity-and-risk`, `housing-and-belonging`  
**Possible chapter:** House or Company

The choice between putting money toward a house deposit and funding a company. Mortgage eligibility rewards predictable income; founding directs capital into a firm and looks like risk. Security and entrepreneurship are forced into opposition.

**Force to preserve:** This is a real bind, not a thought experiment. Societies that demand innovation while making housing depend on avoiding risk are talking out of both sides of their mouths.

## Scene: Eighteen square metres that felt like ours

**Claim type:** personal-experience  
**Topics:** `housing-and-belonging`, `youth-and-work`, `institutions-worth-keeping`  
**Possible chapters:** What Made Them Rich Made Their Children Poor; The Cost of Membership; Youth Without a Ladder; What Is Worth Keeping  
**Source:** `inbox/transcripts/2026-08-05-poseidon-vs-private-rent.md`  
**Active draft:** `scenes/active/eighteen-square-metres.md` → `chapters/what-made-them-rich-made-their-children-poor.md`

First move to Sweden: with partner, into an ~18 m² flat in Guldheden (confirm spelling/history), Poseidon-owned. Toilet in the flat; shower in another building. Quiet, very safe; felt far from the city though close — dead-end tram line. Mezzanine bed, desk underneath, huge window onto grass. Rent later ~1,500 kr/month (lower at start; rose a year later). Then a bigger one-bedroom with lounge for two or three years; happy there until leaving around the financial crash.

**Force to preserve:** Even though we were renting, the places felt like ours. No one was going to take them away. First time in life of feeling free of needing to study or grind — after dropping out in New Zealand. Security without ownership; small, compromised space that still allowed a life.

**Author direction (2026-08-05):** Lead with this positive memory. Proposal: New Zealand should copy this kind of programme; Sweden should expand it. Do not open the current chapter on the later private-market hell.

**Optional / memoir only:** snoring and floor-sleeping; “Bongo Bongo Lady” sound war — see `memoir-vault/guldheden-sleep-and-neighbors.md`. Author unsure about sharing the sleep detail.

## Scene: Back without a first-hand contract

**Claim type:** personal-experience  
**Topics:** `housing-and-belonging`  
**Possible chapters:** The Cost of Membership; Youth Without a Ladder  
**Source:** `inbox/transcripts/2026-08-05-poseidon-vs-private-rent.md`  
**Legal care:** `legal-review/private-landlords-samtrygg.md`

Return to Sweden ~2022 (~12 years later): no Poseidon / first-hand (förstahandskontrakt) / bostads path. At the mercy of the private secondary market — experienced as asset-rich owners renting extras, often without agencies, plus intermediary platforms. Three frustrating private-landlord stories (heater / tea lights / energy bills; bond kept after alleged “trashing”; off-books extension and intermediary pressure). Hyresgästföreningen involved; money still not recovered at time of telling.

**Force to preserve:** Same country, opposite tenure reality. The contrast between first-hand systems and private renting is stark. Anger is earned; named people need careful wording.
